# Couples order a design from the studio

ADR 0007 has a couple approve their design's proof in the studio, then order it on the shop's product page, which asks again what the design already shows: its paper, the seal, the ribbon, the envelope, and the text the couple typed (Text personalizat, which 37 of the 42 products a collection makes require). The studio's own papers and waxes don't match the shop's, so the two can disagree. From now on the studio asks the order beside the design, and approving the proof adds the line to the cart. Microfrontends stay (ADR 0004): a couple crosses into the shop once, at the cart, on the same address.

- **The studio reads the product.** A template names the product it's ordered as (`templates.shopifyProductHandle`), and `catalog.product` gives the studio that product while it's for sale: its price, its minimum order, its papers and the order options its line asks, each paper and choice with what it adds, in bani, as the cart prices them.
- **Each paper and choice has a look**: what choosing it does to a design (`lookSchema` in `packages/design`), so the studio shows what the couple orders. A paper's look is its stock and colour; a choice's is a seal's wax or no seal, a ribbon's material and colour or no ribbon, the envelope's colour, a liner printed with the design or none, or the corners. Choosing one changes the design by operations (`applyLook`), like any other edit, and a design already showing it needs none (`showsLook`). A choice without a look, such as preserved flowers, is ordered in words: the studio doesn't draw it.
- **The team sets a choice's look** on its order option's page in the workshop (ADR 0020), on the options that change a design (`convex/catalog/looks.ts`: the seal's and the envelope's colour, the ribbon, the cord, the envelope's liner, the edges and the corners). Until it does, a choice jolene.ro had takes the look `catalog/looks.ts` gives its name, and a new one shows nothing. A paper's look comes from `catalog/looks.ts` alone, by its name, as only the seed names papers.
- **The design's catalogs have what the shop sells.** Its papers include the smooth 250 g and pearlescent 300 g card jolene.ro sold, and a seal's wax can be a colour of its own, as a choice gives it, besides the studio's waxes.
- **A Finisaje tab asks the order** while the couple designs: the product's papers and order options at the shop's prices, how many, and the total, which the Order button shows too. A choice that shows changes the design at once. Paper and the seal leave the Stil tab and expert mode's inspector, as do the corners when the product's order options pick them, so the studio offers only what the product sells, each in one place, and the Finisaje tab offers all of it. What the design shows answers its options: the design keeps the couple's picks and the quantity (`designs.finishes`), and a pick counts while the design shows it, so an undo or the AI designer changing the seal changes the choice ordered with it. An option none of whose choices the design shows, as Royal Blue's gold envelope is none of the shop's, is left to choose.
- **Approving adds the line.** The Order button opens the design's sheet over it: the proof as ADR 0007 has it, beside the order the Finisaje tab asked, its total and the delivery. One tick approves the proof and puts the design in the cart, after which the sheet offers the collection's other pieces for the same event, which already show the couple's details. The shop keeps the cart, under a cookie of its own, so the studio asks the shop's server to add the line, on the same address (`POST /api/cart/proofs`), and it does so with `cart.addProof`: the proof names its template's product, so the studio sends only the proof, the quantity and the picks. A line with a proof needs no Text personalizat, which the proof answers, and pays no fee for diacritics, which the couple typed themselves. The proof keeps its page, `/studio/design/<id>/bun-de-tipar`, with the same sheet.
- **A cart holds a design once.** Approving a design again after a change puts the new proof and picks on its line, where ADR 0007 added a line of its own and left the old one in the cart.
- **The shop's page for these products leads into the studio**, after its photos: they're ordered through the studio, and a special request through Scrieți-ne. `shop.product` names the template a product is designed from, and Personalizează în studio starts a design of it (`/studio/design/nou/<template>`), which takes the starting page's place, so going back returns to the shop. A link that carries a proof to a product page still adds its line, with a form that asks neither the text nor the diacritics. Every other product keeps its page and form.

## Consequences

- The looks `catalog/looks.ts` gives jolene.ro's choices are guesses from their names, from which the team corrects any on the workshop's page. Six collections' envelopes are one of the shop's envelope colours (Forest, Autumn, Carusel, Classic Roses, Eucalipt and Geometric Flowers), so their designs start with it chosen; Royal Blue's gold, Teddy's sage, Safari's teal and Boho's petrol envelopes are none of them until the team adds one.
- Fringed edges show as square corners, and preserved flowers not at all: they're in the order, not on the design.
- A look the team changes changes what choosing that choice does from then on. A design made before keeps what it shows, and shows the choice whose look it has, if any.
- The print file prints the pieces as before. A seal, a ribbon and the envelope are made from the order's answers, as on jolene.ro.
- Only a page on the shop's own address can add a design to a visitor's cart: the endpoint takes JSON from the same origin alone, which a page elsewhere can't send without CORS, and the cart's cookie isn't sent across sites.
- A link that carries a proof to a product page still adds a line of its own, as ADR 0007 has it; approving that design again in the studio leaves one line for it.
