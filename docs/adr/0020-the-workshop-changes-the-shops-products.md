# The workshop changes the shop's products

The catalog lives in Convex, where the studio's seed loads it from jolene.ro's lift, and a product edited in Convex keeps its edits (ADR 0015). Until now nothing edited one: a new price meant a change to `packages/products/catalog` and a deploy. Jolene's team now changes the shop's products in the studio, at `/studio/atelier/produse` (`convex/workshop/products.ts`), its categories at `/studio/atelier/categorii` (`convex/workshop/categories.ts`), and its order options at `/studio/atelier/optiuni` (`convex/workshop/lineOptions.ts`).

- **What the team changes**: a product's name, its price, the price before a discount, its minimum order, what each of its papers adds, its photos, its categories, the order options it asks, and whether it's for sale. Its description stays as the seed loaded it for now: the shop shows it as HTML, which only a cleaned-up editor should write. A product taken off sale leaves the shop and its categories, a cart that holds it says it's no longer available, and paid orders stay as they are.
- **The team's photos are kept in Convex storage.** The studio makes each one fit for the web before it goes up, as the photo store's copies are (ADR 0016): at most 2048 pixels a side, turned upright, in sRGB and without the camera's metadata, in its own format, a JPEG, PNG or WebP of 10 MB at most. Convex keeps an upload until the product is saved with it (`photoUploads`), and deletes one no product took a day later. A product's photos are saved with the rest of it, in their order: the first is the one lists and the cart show. Taking a team photo off a product deletes it; jolene.ro's stay in the photo store, which other deployments share.
- **Categories and order options are the shop's.** The team picks which of the shop's categories a product is on, and its main one, the first, which its page's path and similar products come from. It picks which of the shop's order options the product asks, in order, and no two that share an answer (their `key`). An option's choices and prices are the same on every product that asks it, so they're changed for all of them, not on one product.
- **The team names, orders and adds the shop's categories.** A category keeps the address it was made with, where jolene.ro's old links and the shop's own lead, so a new name changes only what the shop shows. A new category gets its address from its name: one no other category has, and no product is on. None is deleted, as a product or a link can still lead to it. The list saves as a whole, against the version the page showed (each category's address and name, in order); if someone changed it since, nothing changes (`categories-changed`). Saving it makes every category the team's (`editedAt`, `editedBy`), so the seed leaves their names and order alone; saving it unchanged leaves them the seed's.
- **The team changes the shop's order options, each on every product that asks it.** An option's page changes its name, the line under it, whether it must be answered, and a select's choices, in order, with what each adds to each one ordered. It lists the products that ask it, and the options that share its answer. Its key and its kind stay as the seed made them, as a cart answers an option by its key. A select keeps one choice at least, no two with the same name. It saves against the version the page showed, as a product does (`line-option-changed`), and makes the option the team's (`editedAt`, `editedBy`), so the seed leaves it alone.
- **The shop lists a category once something is for sale on it.** A new one shows after the team puts a product on it, from the product's page; one whose products all go off sale drops out of the shop's list until one is back.
- **A changed product is the team's.** Saving sets `editedAt` and `editedBy`, and the seed leaves the product as the team left it from then on. Saving with nothing changed leaves it the seed's.
- **Nobody overwrites a colleague's change.** The page saves against the version it showed (its `editedAt`); if someone saved the product since, nothing changes (`product-changed`) and the page shows their version. A page with nothing typed in it follows the product as it changes.
- **The shop shows a change on the next visit.** The shop caches what its pages read of the catalog for minutes, under tags (ADR 0015). Once a change is saved, the workshop's page posts the account's Convex token to the shop's `/api/catalog/refresh`, on the same address through the microfrontends group (ADR 0004); the shop asks Convex whether the token is a workshop account's and expires the `products` and `categories` tags. It needs no new secret.

## Consequences

- The refresh can fail, as on a preview, where the studio and the shop have addresses of their own. The page then says the shop shows the change within minutes, as long as its cache keeps the catalog.
- For a product the team has changed, Convex says what the shop sells, not `packages/products/catalog`: a change to it in the repository doesn't reach the shop.
- About 300 products' descriptions state their minimum order ("Comandă minimă: 30 buc."), so a new minimum leaves the old one in the description until descriptions can be changed in the workshop too.
- A cart line keeps the answers the couple gave, priced by the product as it is now. When the team takes an order option off a product, its answer drops off the line, with what it added to the price. One the team adds that must be answered stops the line from being paid for, and the cart asks the couple to add the product again, as it does when an option's choices change.
- A cart prices its lines by the order options as they are now: a new price shows in carts already filled. A choice renamed or taken off, or an option made one that must be answered, stops a line that chose it, or left it empty, from being paid for until the couple adds the product again. Paid orders keep the choices and prices they were paid with.
- Options that share an answer keep their own choices and prices, as jolene.ro had them: a price changed on one isn't changed on the others, which its page lists to change in turn.
- A team photo shows only on the deployment it was uploaded to, like every change the team makes there.
- A category the team no longer wants is emptied rather than deleted: with no product for sale on it, the shop stops listing it, and its address shows an empty shelf.
- A category the seed adds after the team has saved the list goes to its place in the seed's order, among the team's, until the team saves the list again.
